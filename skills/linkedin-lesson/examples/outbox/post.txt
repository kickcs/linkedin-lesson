A lot of problems come from edge cases and outages. We often don't plan for them when we design a service, and start thinking about them only after something breaks in production.

Take a money transfer. The service saves it, then calls the bank. If the service crashes in between, the transfer is in the database, but the bank never gets the request, and Anna gets $0.

To solve this, we can use a transactional outbox. The service saves the transfer and a “pay Anna” note in one write. A separate worker carries the note to the bank until it confirms, and the bank pays each note once. Nothing is lost, but the app hears “accepted” instead of “done”.
