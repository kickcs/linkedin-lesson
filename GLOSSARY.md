# LinkedIn lessons

Short educational posts for LinkedIn: a text and a GIF that explains one idea from AI and software
development.

## Lesson

**GIF**:
A silent looping animated lesson that LinkedIn shows in the feed as an image.
_Avoid_: clip, video

**Viewer**:
A person in the LinkedIn feed who may or may not know the topic. The GIF teaches them the technique
so that anyone gets the key points.
_Avoid_: layperson, audience, developer

**Key point**:
A claim about the technique that the viewer takes away from the GIF without reading the post: why it
is needed, what it gives, how it works.
_Avoid_: idea, conclusion

**Lesson type**:
The question the GIF answers: “why” (a pattern or a trick), “how it works” (a mechanism) or
“how they differ” (a comparison). The type sets the GIF's flow.
_Avoid_: genre, format

## How a lesson is prepared

**Story**:
The lesson, told to the user in plain words in the user's language before the card: type, key points
and the GIF's flow. The card is written only once the user has understood from the story why the
technique is needed.
_Avoid_: narrative, concept

**Lesson card**:
The document the GIF and the post are built from: key points, events, facts with sources, texts.
_Avoid_: brief, specification

## How the GIF is structured

**Scenario**:
One concrete case on the real parts of the system, through which the GIF shows the technique:
“$100 to Anna”, “a shopper asks about open-ear headphones”. A GIF has one scenario.
_Avoid_: framing, example, narrative

**Look**:
How the GIF is drawn: palette, fonts, zones, layouts and the engine that draws them, such as Board
(a chalkboard) or Cards (parts as cards). The user picks one per lesson; the story and the card's
content do not change with it.
_Avoid_: skin, style, theme, variant

**Layout**:
How the GIF frame of a look is split into zones and what lives in which zone.
_Avoid_: template, mockup

**Theme**:
A brand's own colors, fonts and stroke roughness for a look that takes one (Board), on top of its
defaults. The frame's zones and sizes stay the same under any theme.
_Avoid_: skin, style, template

**Pause question**:
A GIF beat where the scene asks the viewer about the outcome and holds a pause before the answer.
_Avoid_: quiz, comprehension check

**Conclusion**:
The last line of the GIF, which stays on screen until the fade.
_Avoid_: summary, ending

## Post

**Transfer**:
A sentence that shows the same trick on another case. It goes into the post only if the paragraph is
unclear without it.
_Avoid_: use-case example

**Analogy**:
A comparison with a familiar thing from another area that leads to the real parts of the system but
does not replace them. Lives in the story; goes into the post text only if the paragraph is unclear
without it; the GIF has none.
_Avoid_: metaphor, bridge
